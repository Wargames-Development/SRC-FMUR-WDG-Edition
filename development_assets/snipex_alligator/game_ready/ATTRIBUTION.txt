Modified for Wargames/FMUR by separating the body, magazine, and bolt and by
converting the original flat materials into a shared texture palette.

The remodel in ../remodel uses this same source. FMUR resized the silhouette
against the pack M82A3, widened the scope rail, added a folded bipod and baked
a new diffuse atlas. The green-tan revision separates the original brake
and adds a newly modeled suppressor based on the supplied visual reference.
Regenerate and install with tools/build_snipex_alligator.py --install.
