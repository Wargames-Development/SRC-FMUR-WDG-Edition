AK-308 remodel for FMUR

Adapted from "Low-Poly AK-308" by TastyTony, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-ak-308-b2538bf6e49a4084ad5ae2adf6a995ca
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original converted meshes and license are in
development_assets/sketchfab_guns/game_ready/AK308.
The remodel retains the early-pattern receiver, fore-end, sights, stock and
brake. It refits the proportions, rebuilds the magazine and diffuse atlas,
backs the ejection port and separates the moving and replaceable groups.

Build and install with Blender 5.2 from the repository root.
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/build_ak308.py -- --install
