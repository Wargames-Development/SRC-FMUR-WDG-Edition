FMUR G36 rifle conversion

Derived from the existing BFMC Gun Pack MG36 model and diffuse texture in this
repository's development runtime. Original runtime model: mpack.mg36TD
(Modelmg36TD.class), original texture: assets/flansmod/skins/MG36.png.
The supplied compiled model does not identify its original author or license.
Existing pack credits and applicable source rights continue to apply; this file
does not grant new rights to the inherited model or texture.

FMUR adaptation retains the original receiver, stock, grip, handguard, controls
and their UV islands. It adds a curved rifle magazine, barrel, muzzle, mounting
rails and marked iron sights. The finish follows the BFMC pack charcoal palette.
The default iron sights use the existing BFMC HK416 front and rear geometry
and their original atlas strip, fitted to the G36 rail.
This is an adaptation, not a claim of original authorship of the whole weapon.

Attachment preview meshes/textures are the existing BFMC pack HOLO, VerticalGrip,
5MWLaser and DarkZone KXFlashHider at their configured runtime scales.
Attachment textures remain separate from the gun atlas; the editable Blender
scene packs the existing pack images used by its hidden comparison objects.
