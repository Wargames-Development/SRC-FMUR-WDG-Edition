BFMC M240B remodel

Body geometry adapted from “M240 (low-poly)” by ScurvyWoof, CC BY 4.0.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/m240-low-poly-55eaa7d87d3c4546af67343b0fcaf266
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The remodel refits the donor's geometry and recolors the surface groups.
The rail, feed box, belt and muzzle group are authored for BFMC.
The BFMC M60-E4 informed the scale and styling of the feed box.
The original M240 inventory sprite and converted donor are archived under
development_assets/sketchfab_guns/game_ready/M240.

Regenerate with tools/build_m240.py using Blender 5.2's bundled Python.
Pass --install to refresh the meshes, diffuse atlas and credit in eclipse/Flan.
