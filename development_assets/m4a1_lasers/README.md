# M4A1 laser guns

The `content_pack` directory adds two separate FMUR heat weapons: `m4a1IntegratedPulse` and `m4a1UpgradedLaser`. Copy it to the instance's `Flan/M4A1 Lasers` directory (or zip its contents with `pack.mcmeta` at the archive root). Build and install this FMUR mod first: the model classes and binary geometry are compiled and packaged in its JAR. FMUR's bundled `ModelM4A1` supplies animation metadata; BFMC Gun Pack supplies the shared M4A1 inventory icon and attachment resources.

The supplied source folders provided each model and its matching 2048-pixel skin. Both guns use FMUR's heat system and internal projectiles, so neither consumes 5.56 magazines. Each gun has its own projectile definition and matching muzzle flash and tracer colour. Internal energy projectiles do not spawn the cosmetic tracer ricochet. The blue pulse fires faster with lower damage, has 360 maximum heat for roughly twice the firing time before overheat, and rejects laser-pointer and barrel attachments. The red laser hits harder with greater spread and random recoil and rejects barrel attachments. The firing and cooling audio originated in the former Star Wars Blasters development pack. Original gun models and textures were credited to invalidsyntax; Toolbox 2.0 conversion and game integration were credited to Wargames Development Group.

In game, check both localized creative-inventory names, first- and third-person models, aiming, allowed grips, blue and red muzzle flashes and tracers, automatic and semi-automatic fire, overheat lockout and cooling. Check that the blue gun rejects laser pointers, both guns reject barrels, and neither laser projectile produces a tracer ricochet after striking terrain. Neither gun should need a magazine; the existing BFMC M4A1 should behave as before.
